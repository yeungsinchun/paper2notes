02

UI clean-up

Before

Role-selection controls above comparison tables encouraged clicking a label just to highlight the matching row. The visual action did not teach an exam decision and competed with the table itself.

→

After

Tables are plain, scannable reference material and wide tables scroll cleanly on a narrow screen. Syllabus links are a quiet “Related DSE” line rather than a chip cloud. Controls remain only where they alter an observable process: an absorber sequence, a cloud-chamber track, a detector input or a finite animation replay.

Quantitative decay

Whole and fractional half-lives, corrected data, \( A = kN \), exponential decay, unit consistency and nuclei from mass.

26.1

Applications and safety

Radiation type, half-life and chemistry are justified for tracers, therapy, gauges, irradiation and C-14. Equivalent dose is separated from effective dose, with targeted α therapy treated as a purposeful exception to diagnostic tracers.

Review focus

Review the pages as an HKDSE student would: scan a table, explain a choice in a full sentence, then open the matching DSE item. In particular, check that:

  • the application table in 26.2 is readable without clicking a decorative row selector;
  • each 25.1 to 26.3 page has an explicit, topic-matched DSE practice panel;
  • every interactive changes a physical condition or replays a process, rather than acting as decoration; and
  • the section maps, detailed pages and summaries feel like one quiet, printable set of notes.
