### HKDSE sap/36
- **Source:** `.../sap_q36.png`

### HKDSE PP q34

- **Tag:** α vs β mass, penetrating, discharge
- **Why:** (1) mass; (2) penetrating is the usual false claim; (3) α ionizes air and can discharge a charged sphere (Example 25.5 family).
