Decayed nuclei are still there

They become a daughter nuclide. Mass does not vanish after one half-life.

Subtract background first

Half-life is read from corrected count rate, not from a graph that still includes background.

Long T½ is not “weak”

Activity also depends on how many nuclei you have. Compare A, not T½ alone.
