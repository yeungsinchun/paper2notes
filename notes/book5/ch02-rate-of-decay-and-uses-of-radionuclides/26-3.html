Concept check

An α source such as Po-210 is not a serious external hazard if it stays outside the body.

The same α source is deadly if it is ingested.

Concept check

Which unit measures the radiation equivalent dose of a patient receiving radiotherapy?

Concept check

The biological effect of radiation depends only on the amount absorbed, not on the type or the organ.

A person receives about 2 Sv of effective dose from the environment in a year.

1 Sv from an α source and 1 Sv from a γ source produce different biological effects, because α is more ionizing.

Hong Kong background is about 2.4 mSv per year. Public limit 1 mSv; radiation workers 20 mSv (those limits exclude natural background and medical procedures).
