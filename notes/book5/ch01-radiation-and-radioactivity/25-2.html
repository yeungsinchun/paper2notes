Background is mostly natural

Fig. 25.21-25.23

Natural ~80%: radon 42% (largest slice), rock/soil 16%, cosmic 13%, food/drink 9%.

Artificial ~20%, almost all medicine in the book's pie.

Irradiation does not make an object radioactive; contamination does.
