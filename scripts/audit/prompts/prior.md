# Prior knowledge allowlist

This is the ONLY physics and maths a solver may use besides the NOTES and the question itself.

## Force and motion (listed force and motion knowledge)

- P-FM-01: distance, displacement, speed, velocity
- P-FM-02: acceleration, equations of uniformly accelerated motion
- P-FM-03: graphical analysis of motion (s-t, v-t, a-t, gradient, area)
- P-FM-04: Newton's first law
- P-FM-05: Newton's second law F=ma
- P-FM-06: Newton's third law
- P-FM-07: weight W=mg
- P-FM-08: friction, normal reaction
- P-FM-09: moment of a force, equilibrium of a rigid body
- P-FM-10: work W=Fs, energy, power P=W/t
- P-FM-11: kinetic energy KE=1/2 mv^2
- P-FM-12: gravitational potential energy PE=mgh
- P-FM-13: conservation of mechanical energy
- P-FM-14: momentum p=mv, impulse Ft
- P-FM-15: conservation of momentum
- P-FM-16: projectile motion (horizontal and vertical components)
- P-FM-17: circular motion (centripetal force, period, frequency)
- P-FM-18: Newton's law of gravitation

## Mathematics (P-MA-*)

- P-MA-01: arithmetic, percentages, ratios
- P-MA-02: algebra, rearranging formulae, simultaneous equations
- P-MA-03: reading and sketching graphs (gradient, area)
- P-MA-04: trigonometry (sin, cos, tan)
- P-MA-05: powers of ten, significant figures, standard form
- P-MA-06: vectors (addition, resolution into components) – where taught in ch2

Logarithms and exponentials are NOT included; Book 5's N=N0e^{-kt} and T½=ln2/k must be taught by the notes.

## Given (P-GIVEN)

Any datum stated in the item itself, including values given in the stem.
